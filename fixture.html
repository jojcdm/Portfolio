01 / Problem & Verification Need

Standardizing rotational catheter verification

During Device Verification Testing (DVT) of experimental electrophysiology cardiac catheters, manual rotational handling introduces operator-to-operator variability, off-axis loading, and inconsistent test setup times across samples.

Working directly with Quality and R&D teams, I identified the testing gap, engineered a dedicated rotating fixture architecture, built and qualified physical prototypes, and developed patent fingerprinting concepts alongside formal disclosure documentation.
